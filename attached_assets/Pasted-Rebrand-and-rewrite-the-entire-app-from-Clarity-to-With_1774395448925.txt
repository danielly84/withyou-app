Rebrand and rewrite the entire app from “Clarity” to “WithYou”, and update all user-facing copy to match a more human, warm, emotionally supportive voice.

Do not rebuild the app. Do not change layout, structure, or core functionality. This is a full copy and tone transformation pass.

Goal:
Shift the product from a “reflection tool” to a “supportive presence that’s with you while you think things through.”

---

1. Rename the product everywhere

Replace “Clarity” with “WithYou” in:
- app header / logo text
- page titles
- browser title
- empty states
- onboarding text
- metadata and labels
- disclaimers

Ensure naming is consistent across the entire app.

---

2. Voice and tone transformation

Rewrite all user-facing copy to feel:
- warm
- human
- calm
- supportive
- present
- non-clinical
- non-robotic

The app should feel like:
- a thoughtful friend
- someone sitting with you
- not a tool analyzing you

Avoid:
- clinical language
- analytical or dashboard-like phrasing
- overly product-y wording
- anything that sounds like diagnosis

Prefer:
- gentle, conversational phrasing
- “I’m here with you” energy
- reflective language over analytical language

---

3. Homepage / entry screen

Keep the structure, but adjust copy:

- Keep or slightly soften headline:
  “How are you feeling today?” (this is still good)

- Add or refine supporting line:
  Example:
  “You don’t have to figure it out alone.”

- Mode toggle labels:
  - “Just vent”
  - “Help me reflect”

- Helper text updates:

For “Just vent”:
“Write whatever’s on your mind. I’ll respond once.”

For “Help me reflect”:
“Share what’s on your mind. I’ll ask one question to help you go a little deeper.”

- Keep tone soft and natural

---

4. Question screen (AI follow-up)

Update tone so it feels like a presence, not a system:

- Keep reflection text human and grounded
- Add or refine intro line:
  “Here’s what I’m hearing”

- Label for question:
  “A question to sit with”

- Helper text above input:
  “When you’re ready…”

- Ensure everything feels like a continuation of a conversation, not a task

---

5. Final response screen

Refine structure and tone:

- Emotion label:
  Instead of system-like phrasing, use:
  “It sounds like you’re feeling ___”

- Section headings:

Replace analytical labels with softer ones:

- “INSIGHT” → “What this might mean”
- “A thought for today” → “Something to try” or “A small step you could take”

- Add a subtle emotional anchor at the end:
  Example:
  “That’s a lot to carry, and it makes sense it feels this way.”

- Ensure all text feels reflective, not diagnostic

---

6. Insights page

Shift from analytics → reflection

- “What I’m noticing” stays, but tone should feel like:
  “Here’s what I’ve been noticing from what you’ve shared”

- Metric cards:
  - “Total Entries” → “You’ve checked in X times”
  - “Most Frequent” → “You’ve been feeling X most often”

- Add soft interpretation lines:
  “This shows up a lot in what you’ve been sharing”

- “Recent Glimpses” → “Recent reflections”

- Keep everything human, not data-heavy

---

7. Feedback UI

Keep:
“Was this helpful?”

But refine tone slightly if needed:
- “Did this feel helpful?”

Ensure it feels like a gentle check-in, not a survey

---

8. Disclaimer (important)

Update to:

“WithYou is here to support reflection and emotional awareness. It isn’t a medical, therapy, or crisis service.”

Keep tone clear but not harsh

---

9. Microcopy consistency pass

Review all visible text and ensure:
- no leftover “Clarity” references
- no clinical or analytical language
- no robotic phrasing
- everything feels cohesive with the “WithYou” voice

---

10. Final quality check

The app should feel like:
- something that sits with you
- not something that evaluates you

When reading any screen, it should feel like:
“I’m not alone in this moment”

---

Important:
This is a copy and tone transformation only. Preserve all functionality, flows, data handling, and layout. Only update language, naming, and emotional tone.
